About Us

Welcome to BigBasket

BigBasket is India’s leading online grocery store providing fresh vegetables, fruits, and daily essentials. Founded with a mission to make grocery shopping easier, faster, and more convenient, we serve millions of happy customers every day across multiple cities.

From farm-fresh produce to household items — we deliver everything at your doorstep, ensuring quality and timely delivery.

Our Mission & Vision

We aim to redefine the way people buy groceries by combining technology, trust, and top-quality products.

Our Mission

To deliver fresh and quality groceries at the best prices, making everyday life easier for our customers through fast and reliable delivery.

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted online grocery platform, providing sustainable and customer-centric services to every household.
